# Problem and Evidence

## Problem statement

Sellers may prepare or acquire more vegetables than buyers take. Remaining stock has a short marketable window, yet information about available produce is scattered across physical stalls, regular-buyer networks, calls, messages, Messenger, and Facebook groups. Buyers may not see the right listing in time or may lack current photos, trustworthy seller information, quantity, and pickup details. Local offices lack consolidated records of surplus and transactions.

## Local research base

- **82 participants:** 38 produce sellers and 41 buyers in the survey, plus 3 key informants.
- **Sites:** Bankerohan Public Market, Agdao Public Market, and Davao Food Terminal Complex.
- **Methods:** seller and buyer surveys, face-to-face semi-structured interviews, approximately three observation visits, and consultations with the City Agriculturist's Office, City Economic Enterprises Office, and DFTC Vendor-Farmers Association.
- **Limit:** selected participants and sites; findings do not represent all Davao City markets.

## Findings that drive design

| Finding in proposal | Product implication |
|---|---|
| All surveyed sellers lowered prices on unsold but marketable vegetables. | Let sellers set and update prices, with clear current pricing. |
| 94.7% of sellers would sell at or near buying price to recover capital. | Support surplus-specific promotion without imposing platform prices. |
| 97.4% reported spoiled and unsold produce reduced weekly take-home income. | Prioritize timely listing visibility and accurate availability. |
| 94.7% were willing to use an app to reach alternative buyers. | Keep seller onboarding and listing entry practical. |
| All 41 buyers would consider excess or visually unattractive vegetables if suitable for cooking and fairly priced. | Show condition as seller-declared, with recent images and transparent price. |
| 95.1% wanted recent photos before travelling. | Require or strongly prompt current listing photos. |
| 97.6% preferred evidence of seller legitimacy. | Surface verification and transaction-linked trust signals. |
| All 41 buyers were willing to self-pickup to avoid delivery fees. | Make location and pickup instructions part of discovery and reservation. |

## Observed causes of surplus

Reduced or cancelled regular orders; variable walk-in demand; seasonal supply; holidays and school breaks; weather; temporary closures; price competition; and changes in buyers' menus, budgets, or remaining inventory.

## Evidence to revisit during beta

Measure listing completion, search success, reservations, completed pickups, expired and cancelled reservations, seller effort to promote, and whether users understand condition and pickup information. [[Acceptance and Evaluation]]
