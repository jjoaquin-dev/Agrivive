# Project Brief

## One-sentence concept

Agrivive helps Davao City vegetable sellers find buyers for **marketable surplus** through structured listings, targeted visibility, 24-hour reservations, and verified self-pickup.

## What counts as surplus

Seller-declared vegetables intended for human consumption that exceed expected or confirmed demand and remain unsold during the intended selling period. The platform does not certify internal freshness or food safety. [[Goals and Boundaries]]

## Who benefits

- **Sellers:** suppliers, supplier-vendors, and retail vendors gain an additional channel for remaining stock, clearer reservation coordination, and a record of surplus activity.
- **Buyers:** households, resellers, and food-related businesses can find suitable vegetables by type, quantity, price, unit, seller, and location, then arrange self-pickup.
- **Authorized stakeholders:** local offices can inspect aggregate, non-personal marketplace trends for monitoring and planning. [[People and Roles]]

## Core value loop

Seller posts marketable surplus → buyer discovers a current listing → buyer reserves an available quantity → system issues a 24-hour QR code → seller validates pickup and handover → stock and descriptive records update → future visibility, recommendations, and insights improve.

[[Product Journeys]] · [[Reservation and QR Pickup]] · [[Seller Analytics]]

## Project frame

- **Location:** Davao City, with fieldwork at Bankerohan Public Market, Agdao Public Market, and Davao Food Terminal Complex.
- **Academic period:** 2026–2027.
- **Method:** iterative Agile development and beta evaluation with selected participants.
- **Interfaces:** React Native seller mobile app; Next.js buyer, administrator, and stakeholder web app.
- **Backend:** ElysiaJS, Better Auth, PostgreSQL, and supporting services. [[Architecture and Integrations]]

## Intended impact

The project aims to make surplus visible while still marketable, reduce scattered communication and pickup uncertainty, and build a usable history of posted, reserved, sold, and recurring surplus. These are intended outcomes, not guarantees of sales or waste reduction.
