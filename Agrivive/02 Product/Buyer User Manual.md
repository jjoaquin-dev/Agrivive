# Agrivive Buyer User Manual

Agrivive helps you reserve fresh vegetables from local market stalls in Davao City and pick them up yourself. This manual shows you every step, from making an account to picking up your order.

> **About this version:** Sections marked **(Coming soon)** describe the new pickup-time and cancel rules. They are planned and approved, but not live yet. Everything else works today.
>
> **A note about quality:** Agrivive does not check how fresh or good any produce is. Look at your produce when you pick it up. If something is wrong, tell the seller and use the Report button.

## 1. Quick start

1. **Sign up** with your email or Google.
2. **Find** produce in the Marketplace.
3. **Add** what you want to your cart.
4. **Reserve** it and choose when you will pick it up.
5. **Show your QR code** at the stall.
6. The seller **scans it**. You take your produce home.

You do not pay on the website. You pay the seller at the stall when you pick up.

---

## 2. Create your account

**With your email**
1. Tap **Register**.
2. Type your name and email.
3. Agrivive sends you a short code by email.
4. Type the code. Your account is ready.

**With Google (on the website)**
1. Tap **Register** or **Sign in**.
2. Choose **Continue with Google**.
3. Pick your Google account.

**Signing in later:** Tap **Sign in**, type your email, and enter the code we send you.

**Tips**
- Use an email you check often. Your codes and notices come to it.
- You can browse the Marketplace without an account. You need an account to reserve.

## 3. Find produce

Open **Marketplace** from the top menu.

### What you see on a product card
- A **photo** and the **name** of the produce.
- The **category** (for example, Root Vegetables) and the **stall name**.
- The **price** and the unit (for example, ₱30 / pile).
- How many are **left**.
- A **discount tag** (for example, 39% OFF) when the seller lowered the price. The old price is crossed out.
- A **heart** to save the item.
- A **quantity box** and the **Add to Cart** button.

### Ways to find what you want
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| Search bar | Type a vegetable name or stall name |
| Category tabs | Show only one kind, such as leafy greens or root vegetables |
| Price filter | Show only items in your price range |
| Distance filter | Show stalls near you |
| Stall on the map | Tap a stall to see its produce |

### Lowest prices for you
At the top of the Marketplace, a row of items priced 30% or more below normal. They are ranked by how new the listing is, how much stock is left, how reliable the seller is, and past listings. This is not a check of quality.

### Sold out items
If an item says **Sold out**, you cannot add it. Follow the seller to hear when it comes back.

## 4. Follow your favorite sellers

Open a seller's stall page and tap **Follow**.

When you follow a seller, you get a notice when they:
- post **new produce**,
- **restock** something,
- **lower a price**. The notice shows the new price, the old price, and how much you save.

To see or remove the sellers you follow:
1. Open your **Profile**.
2. Tap **Followed Sellers**.
3. Tap **Unfollow** next to a seller to stop notices.

You get notices only from the day you start following.

---

## 5. Save items you like

Tap the **heart** on any product card. Your saved items are under **Saved Items** in your profile menu. Tap the heart again to remove it.

---

## 6. Put items in your cart

1. Choose how many you want with the **+** and **−** buttons, or type the number. You can use half amounts, like 0.5 or 2.5.
2. Tap **Add to Cart**.
3. Tap the **cart icon** at the top to open your cart.

In the cart you can:
- change the amount,
- remove an item,
- see your total.

**Several sellers in one cart:** Agrivive splits your cart into **one order for each seller**, because you pick each one up at a different stall. Each order gets its own QR code.

**Not enough stock?** You cannot choose more than what is left.

## 7. Reserve your order

When you are ready, tap **Checkout**.

### What Agrivive checks first
| Check | If it fails |
|---|---|
| Is your account active and not paused? | You see a message with how long the pause lasts |
| Do you have fewer than 3 open reservations? **(Coming soon)** | You are asked to finish one first |
| Is there enough stock? | You are asked to change the amount |

### Choose your pickup time **(Coming soon)**
1. Pick a **day**. You can choose today or up to 2 days ahead (48 hours).
2. Pick a **2-hour time slot**.
3. You only see times when the stall is **open**. Sellers set their own opening hours, so you cannot pick a time when they are closed.

Read the short note on this page. **If you do not pick up, your account can be paused.**

### Until the new pickup time arrives
Today, your reservation is held for **24 hours** from the time you order. After that it expires.

### After you tap Reserve
- The produce is **held for you**. The stock goes down so nobody else can take it.
- You get your **QR code**.
- The seller is told about your order.

## 8. Your QR code

Your QR code is your pickup pass. Find it under **Orders**, then open the order.

### What the QR code looks like
It is a big square code. You can make it larger on your screen so the seller can scan it easily.

### The five states
| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Not yet** **(Coming soon)** | Your pickup time has not started. The page shows the start time |
| **Available** | You can pick up now. Show it to the seller |
| **Completed** | The seller scanned it. You are done |
| **Cancelled** | The order was cancelled |
| **Expired** | The time ran out and nobody picked it up |

### Rules for the QR code
- It works for **one order only**.
- It **cannot be extended**. If you miss your time, the order expires.
- If you order again, you get a **new QR code**.
- Do not share or copy it. Misusing a QR code is a serious violation and can block your account at once.

---

## 9. Pick up your order

1. Go to the stall at your pickup time.
2. Open the order on your phone and show the **QR code**. Turn your screen brightness up.
3. The seller **scans** it.
4. Check your produce. The seller gives it to you. You pay the seller.
5. The order becomes **Completed**.

**If the QR code will not scan**
- Turn your brightness up and hold your phone steady.
- Tilt the screen to avoid glare.
- Ask the seller to try again, or tell them your order number.

**Scanning never gives stock back.** The seller scanning your code only marks your order as picked up.

**If the seller cancels your order:** Agrivive tells you. The stock goes back to the listing. This does not count against you.

## 10. Cancel an order

You can cancel an order you no longer need. Please do it as early as you can so the seller can sell to someone else.

### How to cancel **(Coming soon)**
1. Open your order under **Orders**.
2. Tap **Cancel**.
3. **Pick a reason** from the list, such as "I changed my mind" or "I cannot make it". Choose **Other** to type your own.
4. Tap to confirm. The **seller sees your reason**.
5. The seller confirms. If they do not answer within **6 hours** (or before your pickup window ends, whichever is first), the cancel is **approved automatically**.
6. The stock goes back to the seller.

### Does a cancel count against me?
- If you cancel **more than 3 hours before your pickup time**, it does **not** count.
- If you cancel **within 3 hours of your pickup time** (or after it starts), it **counts** toward the cancel rules.

Today, cancelling is instant and every cancel counts.

See [the trust rules](#15-the-trust-rules) to learn what happens when cancels build up.

## 11. Talk to a seller

You can message a seller about a product or an order.

1. Open a product or an order and tap **Message seller**.
2. Or open **Messages** from the top menu.

**In the Messages page you can**
- search your chats,
- see unread chats,
- open the **three dots (⋯)** at the top to **View Archived** or **View Blocked** chats,
- use the **three dots on a chat** to **Archive** or **Block** it,
- tap **Profile** in the seller panel to see their stall.

**Archive** hides a chat but keeps it. **Block** stops messages from that seller. You can unblock later.

**How fast should a seller answer?** Agrivive reminds the seller at 12 hours, warns them at 24 hours (and tells you), and flags them at 48 hours.

## 12. Notifications

Tap the **bell** at the top of the page.

### What you will be told about
| Notice | When |
|---|---|
| New produce, restock, or price drop | A seller you follow posts something new |
| Seller replied | A seller answers your question, replies to your review, or sends you a message |
| Reservation ending soon | Your pickup time is almost over |
| Reservation cancelled | The seller cancelled your order |
| Seller has not replied | A seller has not answered you after 24 hours |
| Warning, pause, or block | Your cancel or missed-pickup rules were reached |
| Account update | Other news about your account |

**Tabs:** View all, Orders, Produce, and Updates.

**Buttons:** Tap a notice to open it. Use **Mark all as read** to clear the dots. Open **Manage notifications** to change what you get. Open **View all notifications** for the full list.

## 13. Rate a seller and report a problem

### Leave a rating
After your order is **Completed**, open it and rate the seller from 1 to 5 stars. You can add words. The seller may reply to your review, and you get a notice.

### Report a problem
If something went wrong with an order:
1. Open the order and tap **Report**.
2. Choose a **reason**.
3. Write what happened (at least a few words). You can add a photo as proof.
4. Send it. You can report each order **once**.

| Report reason | What it means |
|---|---|
| Listing was not accurate | The produce was not what the listing said |
| Conduct | The seller was rude or acted badly |
| QR misuse | Someone misused a QR code |
| Pickup problem / Other | For your record. These do not add points |

### What happens next
1. Your report is saved. **Nothing happens to the seller yet.** One report alone is only a claim.
2. If **2 different people** report the **same reason** about the **same seller**, it becomes a confirmed violation.
3. Confirmed violations add points to the seller.

Reports are taken seriously. Please report only real problems.

## 15. The trust rules

Sellers plan their stock around your reservations. These rules protect them, and protect you from sellers who do not reply. All counts look back over the **last 90 days**.

### If you cancel **(Coming soon)**
Only cancels made **within 3 hours of your pickup time** (or later) count.

| Counted cancels | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1 | Nothing |
| 2 | Warning |
| 3 | Reservations paused for 12 hours |
| 4 | Paused for 24 hours |
| 5 | Paused for 3 days |
| 6 | **Account blocked** |

### If you do not pick up (the order expires) **(Coming soon)**
| Expired orders | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1 | Notice only |
| 2 | Notice, then paused 24 hours |
| 3 | Notice, then paused 48 hours |
| 4 | Notice, then paused 3 days |
| 5 | Notice, then **account blocked** |

### Both together
If your counted cancels **plus** your expired orders reach **8**, your account is blocked.

### What "paused" means
You can still browse, message sellers, and use your profile. You **cannot reserve** until the pause ends. It ends by itself. The notice tells you how much time is left.

### What "blocked" means
You cannot sign in or reserve. **You cannot restore a blocked account yourself.** An admin can review a mistake. Please cancel early and pick up on time to avoid this.

### Other things that can block your account
- A confirmed **QR misuse** blocks you right away.
- **No activity for 30 days.** You get a reminder at 3 weeks.

### How to stay safe
- Reserve only what you will pick up.
- Pick a time you can really make.
- If your plans change, cancel **early**, and pick a reason.
- Message the seller if you are running late.

### Today's rules
Until the new rules start, a missed pickup adds 2 points, and cancels are counted on their own. See your notices for warnings.

## 16. Help and common questions

**Do I pay on the website?**
No. You pay the seller at the stall when you pick up.

**Can I change my pickup time after I reserve?** **(Coming soon)**
No. Cancel early and reserve again.

**Why can't I reserve?**
Your account may be paused, you may have 3 open reservations, or the item may be sold out. The message on the screen tells you which.

**Why can't I add more of an item?**
You cannot add more than what is left.

**I lost my phone and cannot use 2FA.**
Use one of your saved backup codes.

**A seller does not answer me.**
Agrivive reminds the seller at 12 hours, warns at 24 hours, and flags at 48 hours. You get a notice at 24 hours.

**My order says Expired but I was on my way.**
The time ran out. Tell the seller. Next time, message them if you will be late.

**Does Agrivive check if the produce is fresh?**
No. Check your produce at pickup. If it was not as described, report it.

**Which device can I use?**
Use the website on any phone or computer. The Agrivive mobile app is for sellers.

## 17. Why these rules exist

This section explains where the rules come from, in simple words. The full list of sources is in *Research Basis for QR and Trust Rules*.

### Where the rules come from
| Part | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| Reminder at 12 hours, warning at 24 hours, flag at 48 hours for sellers who do not reply | The Agrivive research paper (Table 17) |
| Adding up points and comparing them to levels (warning, pause, block) | The Agrivive research paper (Equation 3 and Table 18) |
| The exact points and levels, the 90-day window, and the cancel and missed-pickup steps | Settings chosen by the Agrivive team. They can be changed after we see how people really use the app |

No study gives one correct set of numbers. These numbers are a starting point and may change.

### Why penalties grow step by step
Studies of groups that share things fairly, such as the work of Elinor Ostrom on shared resources, found that rules work best when the first mistake gets a small response and repeated mistakes get a bigger one. That is why you first get a notice, then a warning, then a pause, and only last a block.

### Why a report alone does nothing
Reports are useful, but one report can be a mistake, or can be used unfairly against a seller. Research on how people use "flag" buttons shows they are a blunt tool that can be abused. So Agrivive waits until **two different people** report the **same thing** about the same seller.

### Why you get clear notices
People should understand why something happened to their account. Good design advice (Jakob Nielsen) says to keep people informed and to explain problems in plain words. Studies of restaurants also found that strict cancellation rules are accepted better when they are explained and seen as fair. That is why Agrivive shows warnings at checkout and sends a notice each time a rule is reached.

### Why a QR code works only once and only for a short time
A pickup code that works only once, and only for a short time, is a common safety practice. It stops copied or old codes from being used. Research also shows that people scan codes without checking them, so Agrivive checks every code on the server before anything happens.

### What the rules cannot do
- They do not check how fresh produce is.
- They cannot prove who is right in an argument. They only use events the system can see, such as a missed pickup or a late reply.
- A pause or block is the result of counts in the last 90 days, not a judgment about you as a person.
