# Product Journeys

## Seller: from surplus to completed pickup

1. Register and complete seller verification.
2. Create a listing with product, unit, price, quantity, current photo, declared condition, inventory and storage information, availability, and pickup location.
3. Review listing visibility and prepared promotional content; share when appropriate.
4. Answer inquiries and watch available and reserved quantities.
5. Receive a reservation notice and coordinate self-pickup.
6. Scan the buyer's active QR code, verify the reservation, and confirm handover.
7. Review completed sales, remaining stock, recurring surplus, and relevant advisories.

## Buyer: from discovery to collection

1. Register or browse permitted public content.
2. Search and filter by vegetable, quantity, price, selling unit, seller type, and location.
3. Compare recent photos, seller-declared condition, available stock, price, verification, distance, and pickup instructions.
4. Ask a seller a question if needed.
5. Reserve an available quantity and receive a QR code valid for 24 hours.
6. View the reservation countdown and status; cancel while eligible.
7. Travel to the seller, present the QR code, and pay directly during pickup.
8. Submit a rating, review, or transaction-related report where eligible.

## Exceptions that need explicit screens

- No available quantity; listing deactivated; seller updates price or condition.
- Reservation expires or is cancelled, restoring reserved stock.
- QR already used, invalid, expired, or scanned for the wrong reservation.
- Seller or buyer is offline, or an external map, weather, or holiday service fails.
- Pickup does not occur; a report is submitted with evidence.

The sample prototype in the PDF shows login, registration, marketplace, seller map, product reservation, reservation list and QR, dashboard, and messaging screens. These are design references, not evidence of a completed interface.
