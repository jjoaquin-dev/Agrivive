Where each of the four ranking criteria comes from. The method itself (AHP, TOPSIS) is cited from 2022 to 2026 sources only (below). Saaty (1987) and Hwang and Yoon (1981) are the original method papers; the paper no longer cites them. The weights are Agrivive prototype settings.

## Survey-based criteria

Posting age and price drop come from the survey of buyers. Survey set-up (82 participants, 41 buyers): [[Problem and Evidence]]. Add the exact survey result for these two items here when it is at hand.

## Research-based criteria (2022 to 2025)

Numbers are the paper's reference numbers.

- Stock level: [29] "Managing Perishability in the Fruit and Vegetable Supply Chains", Sustainability, 2022 (separates inventory age from inventory quantity).
- Seller reliability: [56] Kokkodis, Lappas and Kane, Production and Operations Management, 2022 (ratings tied to verified purchases).
- Ranking and who gets seen: [28] Derakhshan, Golrezaei, Manshadi and Mirrokni, Management Science, 2022.
- Pricing of perishables, supports the price drop rule: [62] Hasiloglu-Ciftciler and Kaya, 2025; [63] Kayikci et al., Journal of Cleaner Production, 2022.

Also in [[References]]: Chu and Chen (2022) on trust scoring, and He, Hollenbeck and Proserpio (2022) on fake reviews. Check each entry against the publisher page before citing.
