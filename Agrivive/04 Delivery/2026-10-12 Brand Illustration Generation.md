# Brand Illustration Generation — 2026-10-12

## What changed and why

Generated 6 original, consistent brand illustrations for Agrivive's landing page using modern editorial cut-paper aesthetic with soft 3D depth, rounded organic shapes, and friendly vegetable characters (tomatoes, leafy greens, squash, sitaw, carrots).

The hero image was generated first and used as a style and character reference for all subsequent illustrations to ensure complete visual, lighting, palette, and character design consistency.

## Brand assets generated

All assets are located in `web/public/brand/`:
1. `agrivive-market-day-hero.png` (4:3 aspect ratio, wide hero with generous negative space on left for HTML headline & actions).
2. `agrivive-seller-listing.png` (1:1 square aspect ratio, local seller organizing produce with cheerful vegetables).
3. `agrivive-buyer-reserve.png` (1:1 square aspect ratio, buyer with produce basket and abstract reservation checkmark card).
4. `agrivive-pickup.png` (1:1 square aspect ratio, friendly handoff at market stall with produce basket and checkmark motif).
5. `agrivive-market-pulse.png` (1:1 square aspect ratio, vegetables gathered around an abstract upward trend line for Market Pulse).
6. `agrivive-welcome-basket.png` (16:9 wide aspect ratio, full woven basket of vegetables at a welcoming market stall for bottom CTA).
