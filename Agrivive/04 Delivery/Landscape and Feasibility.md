# Landscape and Feasibility

## Related systems in the proposal

| System | Relevant pattern | Gap Agrivive targets |
|---|---|---|
| Facebook Marketplace | General local listings and Messenger coordination | No dedicated structured surplus workflow or seller-level recurrence record |
| eKadiwa | Government-supported agricultural ordering | General program-based selling rather than independent time-sensitive surplus |
| DELIVER-e | Structured agricultural commerce and logistics | Supply-chain focus rather than daily individual seller surplus |
| Ruri Club | Rescue Buy and Group Buy campaigns | Organization-managed campaigns rather than individual seller history |
| MooMart | Farmer-to-business sourcing | Regular B2B sourcing rather than small and changing local surplus |

Agrivive's proposed combination is localized surplus listings, current quantity and condition information, self-pickup reservations, QR handover, promotion, seller-level descriptive records, and aggregate monitoring. This is a proposal comparison, not an independently verified current-market assessment.

## Feasibility summary

- **Operational:** user needs align with existing seller and buyer practices, but onboarding and digital literacy remain adoption risks.
- **Technical:** selected frameworks and services can support a beta, subject to integration, connectivity, device, and external-service constraints.
- **Economic:** most core frameworks are open source; the document estimates **₱185,309** development cost and **₱19,908** annual operating cost. Hosting, domain, maintenance, and usage-based service quotations need validation before deployment.
- **Schedule:** the academic plan uses overlapping Agile phases through December 2026; prioritize approved scope when delays occur.
