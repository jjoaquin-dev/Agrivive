# Agrivive: Master References and Theoretical Foundations

Complete bibliographic citations, academic papers, regulatory frameworks, interface laws, open-source services, and engineering artifacts that ground the Agrivive platform and the Seller Mobile App.

## A. Multicriteria Ranking Method (AHP and TOPSIS)

1. **Saaty, R. W. (1987).** The analytic hierarchy process: what it is and how it is used. *Mathematical Modelling*, 9(3–5), 161–176. [https://doi.org/10.1016/0270-0255(87)90473-8](https://doi.org/10.1016/0270-0255(87)90473-8)  
   *Role in Agrivive:* Used for pairwise comparison matrices, the 1–9 fundamental scale of absolute numbers, calculating priority weight vectors, and validating matrix consistency ($CR < 0.10$).

2. **Hwang, C.-L., & Yoon, K. (1981).** *Multiple Attribute Decision Making: Methods and Applications, A State-of-the-Art Survey*. Lecture Notes in Economics and Mathematical Systems, vol. 186. Springer-Verlag, Berlin Heidelberg. [https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-48318-9](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-48318-9)  
   *Role in Agrivive:* Used for vector normalization of criteria values, determining positive-ideal ($A^+$) and negative-ideal ($A^-$) solutions, Euclidean distance measurements, and calculating relative closeness scores ($C_i^*$).

3. **Ustaoglu, E., Sisman, S., & Aydinoglu, A. C. (2021).** Determining agricultural suitable land in peri-urban geography using GIS and Multi Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA) techniques. *Ecological Modelling*, 455, 109610. [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecolmodel.2021.109610](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecolmodel.2021.109610)  
   *Role in Agrivive:* Domain precedent for integrating geospatial factors and multi-criteria evaluation methods in agricultural analysis.

4. **Krisnawijaya, N. N. K., Tekinerdogan, B., Catal, C., & van der Tol, R. (2023).** Multi-criteria decision analysis approach for selecting feasible data analytics platforms for precision farming. *Computers and Electronics in Agriculture*, 209, 107869. [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compag.2023.107869](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compag.2023.107869)  
   *Role in Agrivive:* Justifies the use of multi-criteria decision models for automated agricultural decision-support systems.

5. **Biswas, T., Majumder, A., Dey, S., Mandal, A., Ray, S., Kapoor, P., Emam, W., Kanthal, S., Ishizaka, A., & Matuka, A. (2024).** Evaluation of management practices in rice–wheat cropping system using multicriteria decision-making methods in conservation agriculture. *Scientific Reports*, 14, Article 8600. [https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-58022-w](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-58022-w)  
   *Role in Agrivive:* Validates hybrid AHP-TOPSIS methodologies for complex agricultural optimization scenarios.

6. **Duarte, A. D. (2024).** Revisão sistemática do método TOPSIS e suas aplicações na agricultura. *Revista Produção Online*, 24(2), Article 5245. [https://doi.org/10.14488/1676-1901.v24i2.5245](https://doi.org/10.14488/1676-1901.v24i2.5245)  
   *Role in Agrivive:* Systematic review demonstrating the viability, sensitivity, and mathematical validity of TOPSIS in agricultural produce systems.

7. **Dodevska, Z., Radovanović, S., Petrović, A., & Delibašić, B. (2023).** When fairness meets consistency in AHP pairwise comparisons. *Mathematics*, 11(3), Article 604. [https://doi.org/10.3390/math11030604](https://doi.org/10.3390/math11030604)  
   *Role in Agrivive:* Theoretical basis for enforcing fairness in criteria weights while maintaining strict consistency ratio constraints ($CR < 0.10$).

8. **Hsiao, L.-S., Huang, C.-J., Liu, H.-T., & Lin, I.-L. (2025).** An AHP-based assessment of the relative importance of risk factors in project management: Designing a bid preparation checklist. *Systems*, 13(5), Article 328. [https://doi.org/10.3390/systems13050328](https://doi.org/10.3390/systems13050328)  
   *Role in Agrivive:* Methodological precedent for deriving factor priority weights through structured expert and stakeholder consensus.

9. **Derakhshan, M., Golrezaei, N., Manshadi, V., & Mirrokni, V. (2022).** Product ranking on online platforms. *Management Science*, 68(6), 4024–4041. [https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2021.4044](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2021.4044) *(Capstone Ref 28)*  
   *Role in Agrivive:* Mathematical foundation for online platform visibility ranking, ensuring merit-based product positioning without pay-to-win manipulation.

> **Note on Prototype Ranking Weights:** The literature above validates the mathematical framework of AHP and TOPSIS. The specific criteria weights (Freshness 40%, Stock Left 30%, Seller Reliability 20%, Repeat Selling 10%) are Agrivive prototype settings established for initial field trials. If required, pairwise surveys with local agricultural stakeholders can formally recalibrate these weights.

## B. Trust, Flagging, and Marketplace Governance

1. **Kokkodis, M., Lappas, T., & Kane, G. C. (2022).** Optional purchase verification in e-commerce platforms: More representative product ratings and higher quality reviews. *Production and Operations Management*, 31(7), 2943–2961. [https://doi.org/10.1111/poms.13731](https://doi.org/10.1111/poms.13731) *(Capstone Ref 56)*  
   *Role in Agrivive:* Empirical basis for purchase-verified feedback. Only completed QR-scanned pickups grant review access; unverified buyer allegations carry 0 penalty points until corroborated.

2. **Chu, W., & Chen, H. (2022).** The customer trust evaluation of e-commerce market based on portfolio weighting: Cloud model. *International Journal of Environment and Pollution*, 71(1–2), 126–140. [https://doi.org/10.1504/IJEP.2022.132356](https://doi.org/10.1504/IJEP.2022.132356) *(Capstone Ref 57)*  
   *Role in Agrivive:* Multi-attribute portfolio weighting model adapted to score composite seller trust from recorded transaction infractions.

3. **Jiao, R., Przepiorka, W., & Buskens, V. (2021).** Reputation effects in peer-to-peer online markets: A meta-analysis. *Social Science Research*, 95, Article 102522. [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ssresearch.2020.102522](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ssresearch.2020.102522) *(Capstone Ref 54)*  
   *Role in Agrivive:* Explains reputation dynamics, transactional trust, and transparency requirements in peer-to-peer commerce.

4. **He, S., Hollenbeck, B., & Proserpio, D. (2022).** The market for fake reviews. *Marketing Science*, 41(5), 896–921. [https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2022.1353](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2022.1353) *(Capstone Ref 55)*  
   *Role in Agrivive:* Safeguards against malicious reporting; informs the requirement of dual-reporter corroboration or verifiable system event logs before penalties apply.

5. **Jiang, Y., Bian, B., & Li, L. (2019).** Integrated harvest and farm-to-door distribution scheduling with postharvest quality deterioration for vegetable online retailing. *Agronomy*, 9(11), Article 724. [https://doi.org/10.3390/agronomy9110724](https://doi.org/10.3390/agronomy9110724) *(Capstone Ref 58)*  
   *Role in Agrivive:* Models physiological quality decay kinetics in fresh produce, justifying the 12-hour freshness curve and 24-hour reservation cancellation rule.

6. **OECD (2022).** *The role of online marketplaces in protecting and empowering consumers*. OECD Digital Economy Papers, No. 334. OECD Publishing, Paris. [https://doi.org/10.1787/9d8cc586-en](https://doi.org/10.1787/9d8cc586-en)  
   *Role in Agrivive:* International regulatory framework for progressive marketplace enforcement: friendly reminder $\to$ formal warning $\to$ temporary restriction $\to$ suspension.

7. **World Intellectual Property Organization (2023, November).** *Study on the practices of e-commerce marketplaces to combat illicit trade online* (Document WIPO/ACE/16/11). WIPO Advisory Committee on Enforcement, Geneva. [https://www.wipo.int/edocs/mdocs/enforcement/en/wipo_ace_16/wipo_ace_16_11.pdf](https://www.wipo.int/edocs/mdocs/enforcement/en/wipo_ace_16/wipo_ace_16_11.pdf)  
   *Role in Agrivive:* Empirical data showing that 86% of international marketplaces use progressive sanction scales.

> **Note on Prototype Trust Thresholds:** Academic research supports the weighted penalty architecture and progressive sanction tiers (warning, pause, block). The exact numbers (1, 2, 3 points; 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 thresholds; 12h/24h/48h reply timers; 21/30-day inactivity) are Agrivive prototype rules designed for field trials and can be tuned after beta testing.

## C. Cognitive Psychology, Ergonomics, and UI/UX Laws

1. **Nielsen, J., & Molich, R. (1990).** Heuristic evaluation of user interfaces. In *Proceedings of the SIGCHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems (CHI '90)* (pp. 249–256). ACM. [https://doi.org/10.1145/97243.97281](https://doi.org/10.1145/97243.97281)  
   *Role in Agrivive:* Principles of Jakob's Law (familiar cart & checkout mental models), Recognition Over Recall (prominent status badges), and Visibility of System Status.

2. **Krug, S. (2014).** *Don't Make Me Think, Revisited: A Common Sense Approach to Web Usability*. New Riders / Peachpit Press.  
   *Role in Agrivive:* Frictionless presentation of the Digital Pickup Pass, clear single-purpose layouts, and self-evident next actions.

3. **Norman, D. A. (2013).** *The Design of Everyday Things: Revised and Expanded Edition*. Basic Books, New York.  
   *Role in Agrivive:* Tactile affordances and signifiers for touch interaction, clear active/disabled button states, and immediate physical feedback.

4. **Fitts, P. M. (1954).** The information capacity of the human motor system in controlling the amplitude of movement. *Journal of Experimental Psychology*, 47(6), 381–391. [https://doi.org/10.1037/h0055392](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0055392)  
   *Role in Agrivive:* Sizing primary action buttons to at least 48px height, icon controls to 44×44px, and deliberately separating destructive controls from confirmations.

5. **Miller, G. A. (1956).** The magical number seven, plus or minus two: Some limits on our capacity for processing information. *Psychological Review*, 63(2), 81–97. [https://doi.org/10.1037/h0043158](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0043158)  
   *Role in Agrivive:* Chunking order details with whitespace and cards, limiting mobile bottom navigation to 5 core destinations, and segmenting order statuses.

6. **Kahneman, D., & Fredrickson, B. L. (1993).** Hedonic evaluation of experience over time: When do people call an experience painful or enjoyable? *Psychological Science*, 4(1), 45–55. [https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.1993.tb00556.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.1993.tb00556.x)  
   *Role in Agrivive:* Peak-End Rule applied to celebratory completion states upon successful QR pickup pass scanning and order handover.
