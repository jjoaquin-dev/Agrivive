# How Agrivive Works: narration script

Voice: a woman, warm and upbeat, a little playful. Smile in the voice, not in a sing-song.

## Pronunciation

| Word | Say it as | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Agrivive | **Ag-ree-vyve** | Stress "AG". "Ag" like "agriculture", "ree" like "free", "vyve" rhymes with "five". |

## Timed lines

Each line must finish before the next scene starts. Fit about 2.5 words per second.

| Time | Scene | Line | Words | Delivery |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.5 - 3.0 | Heading reveals | Here's how Ag-ree-vyve works! | 5 | Bright, a little excited |
| 3.1 - 6.5 | 01 SOURCE | Step one! Farmers share their surplus veggies. | 7 | Lift on "Step one" |
| 6.7 - 10.1 | 02 DISCOVER | Step two! Browse fresh produce and check stock. | 8 | Playful, like pointing at a shelf |
| 10.3 - 13.9 | 03 COLLECT | Step three! Show your QR code and grab your veggies! | 9 | Joyful, slightly faster on "grab your veggies" |
| 14.0 - 15.0 | Final frame | Fresh food, second chances! | 4 | Warm, unhurried, then stop |

## Full script (read in order)

> Here's how Ag-ree-vyve works!
>
> Step one! Farmers share their surplus veggies.
>
> Step two! Browse fresh produce and check stock.
>
> Step three! Show your QR code and grab your veggies!
>
> Fresh food, second chances!

## Notes for recording

- Keep each line short. If a line runs long, cut a word before you speed up the voice.
- Leave a little silence between lines so each step lands with its visual.
- Don't add product names, prices or claims that the section doesn't show.
